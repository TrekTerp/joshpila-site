How to play

Build a wall in both directions from the pointer. When a wall reaches an existing boundary, it becomes solid. Empty chambers fill automatically.

A ball hitting a growing wall costs one life. Any half already completed stays in place. Clear at least 75% to advance.

Each level adds one ball, up to 50. Lives reset to the ball count. Every level uses the full rectangular chamber. Wall effects apply to the outer boundary, completed half-walls, and all newly built walls.

Right-click or Space rotates the builder. Arrow keys move it, Enter builds. F2 starts a new game. F3 or P pauses. Slow speed runs at half speed and awards half points.

Evolved adds particle glow, fading impact lights, a connection pulse when each half-wall anchors, and a short sweep across newly captured territory. Completed walls and captured space share one finish.

The Classic and Evolved buttons change the appearance during a run.

A browser recreation of Dima Pavlovsky’s 1992 game. Scoring bonuses and timing are approximations of the original.
