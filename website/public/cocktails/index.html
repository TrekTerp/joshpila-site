Advent of Cocktails

A December tradition.

My photographs and notes, alongside the recipes that inspired them.

What this is

Every December since 2020, the r/cocktails subreddit has run Advent of Cocktails: one cocktail revealed each day from December 1 through 24, organized by u/robborow. Each day's post gives the drink's history and one or more published specs, usually drawn from named books, bars, or bartender channels. Riffs and variations are explicitly encouraged. Day 5 is a recurring "Repeal Day" theme, and later years lean increasingly on cross-referencing earlier years' cocktails.

My participation

I started making and photographing these cocktails in 2022. This archive brings together my photographs and notes from those Decembers, alongside the published recipes.

2020 and 2021 have recipes but few photographs. I didn’t participate in those years. I plan to make and photograph the earlier drinks as I work through them.

What's in the archive

  • 144 cocktail entries, with recipes and variations, plus links to their sources.
  • 129 photos from my own attempts, matched to the specific day/recipe they document.
  • Where I substituted a different drink for the day's official one (usually for a missing ingredient), both the official recipe and what I actually made are recorded.

Sources & credits

Advent of Cocktails is organized by u/robborow on r/cocktails. The recipes come from the bartenders, bars, books, and channels named in the entries. Each cocktail links to its source post for further background and discussion. The photographs and notes about what I made are my own.
