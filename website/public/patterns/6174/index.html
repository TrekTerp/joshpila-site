The road to 6174

Arrange four digits from largest to smallest, reverse them, and subtract. Repeat, keeping leading zeros. All 9,990 starts with at least two different digits reach 6174 within seven steps.

Drag any highlighted path left or right to change its starting number. Hover or tap a ribbon to inspect it. Where paths overlap, select the field for the one you want to drag.
